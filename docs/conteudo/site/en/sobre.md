# About HelloSquad, the AI marketing team

**A complete marketing team for small businesses, with the decisions in the owner's hands.**

HelloSquad is an AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses. Seven specialists share the brand's memory, organize the work and deliver professional material. The business owner says what's needed and decides what goes out into the world.

## Why HelloSquad exists

Marketing a small business takes strategy, creative, content, ads, customer relationships, numbers and coordination. With separate tools, the owner has to split up the tasks, pick the tools, remember the context and follow up on every step. That leaves little time for the business itself.

HelloSquad brings all of that together in one team that talks to each other, knows the brand and takes on the work, with clear limits and authorizations you control.

## What changes for you

- From scattered tasks to organized work, each piece with a lead specialist.
- From one-off requests to history, context and versions that stay saved.
- From disconnected tools to a team that coordinates the flow.
- From rushed marketing to recurring responsibilities handed off with control.
- From loose numbers to readings and recommendations based on real data.

## The principles HelloSquad follows

1. **Less tooling, more team.** You ask for goals; you don't have to manage commands or agents.
2. **Quality before volume.** A generic or unusable delivery isn't done.
3. **Coordination without a gatekeeper.** Nexo organizes, but you can talk directly to any specialist.
4. **Real specialization.** Each specialist has a distinct responsibility and written limits.
5. **Shared context.** Brand, history and decisions travel with the work, with no new brief.
6. **Controlled autonomy.** Silence isn't approval. Permissions are explicit, limited, revocable and logged.
7. **Transparency.** The squad always tells apart a suggested idea, prepared material, approved content, an authorized action, a confirmed execution and a measured effect.
8. **Proactive, with judgment.** Alerts and suggestions show up because of evidence or an agreed routine, not to look busy.

## What HelloSquad doesn't promise

HelloSquad doesn't promise guaranteed revenue growth and never simulates the results of an action that didn't happen. It doesn't do sales support, negotiate deals or run your whole company. Its commitment is a different one: organized work, professional deliveries, honest numbers and decisions in your hands.
