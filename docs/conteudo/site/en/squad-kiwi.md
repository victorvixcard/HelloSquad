# Kiwi, AI Growth Marketing

**"I grow your business with clear criteria, within the cap you authorize."**

Kiwi is the Growth Marketing specialist on HelloSquad's squad, the AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses, and she brings AI growth marketing to paid and organic acquisition: ads, search engines, conversion, testing and optimization. Not a cent of ad money is spent without your authorization.

## What Kiwi does

Kiwi speaks in numbers and sources. Instead of "reach is low," she tells you which metric changed, by how much, over what period and where the data came from. She proposes the cap before she proposes the action, and she always keeps three things apart: what is a reading, what is a recommendation and what would be an external action.

Planning isn't spending. An assessment isn't permission to change your account. Every change to an ad needs your permission, with a spending cap, a scope and an expiration date.

## What Kiwi delivers

| Delivery | What you get |
|---|---|
| Media plan and budget recommendation | Goals, channels, proposed limits and hypotheses, without spending anything. |
| Ad audiences and tests | Targeting, messages, variations and a test plan for you to approve. |
| Review of existing campaigns | A reading of real metrics from the accounts you authorized, with period, source and limitations. |
| Ad account audit | An analysis of the connected account's structure, targeting and creatives, with opportunities to improve. |
| Ongoing optimization of authorized campaigns | Cycles of analysis and budget or targeting adjustments, only within the limits you approved. |
| Creating, launching or changing ads | Changes applied only with explicit permission, an approved cap and a record of every action. Kiwi confirms through the ad platform's response, not the intention. |
| SEO assessment and strategy (website and local SEO) | An organic search audit covering technical and on-page factors, keywords and local opportunities, in a prioritized plan. |
| Conversion and funnel optimization | An analysis of steps, drop-off, pages and calls to action, with measurable improvements and defined tests. |
| Growth experiments | Hypothesis, test, success criteria and an optimization routine, with Íris reading the results. |

## How Kiwi works with the rest of the squad

Kiwi puts into practice what Bia defines: positioning and the strategic audience belong to Bia. The ads use Kaia's visuals and Otto's copy. In organic search, Kiwi sets the strategy and the keywords, and Otto writes the articles. Íris reads the test results.

## What Kiwi doesn't do

- She doesn't spend on, create, launch or change any ad without permission and an approved cap.
- She doesn't change a campaign "just to test" without permission.
- She doesn't invent rates, costs or results, and she doesn't attribute a sale to an ad without attribution evidence.
- She doesn't set positioning or the strategic audience. That's Bia.
- She doesn't write the article or design the visual. That's Otto and Kaia.

## Fictitious example: Trilha Urbana in search

*Fictitious example: the brand and the numbers are made up.* Trilha Urbana is a shop for city bikes and e-bikes in Curitiba, Paraná.

1. The owner asks Kiwi: "Why doesn't my website show up in search?"
2. Kiwi asks to connect the search engine data. Without it, she explains what she can assess from the public website alone and what stays out of reach.
3. With the data connected, she delivers a prioritized plan: two technical fixes, the pages missing for e-bike searches in the city and the shop's local profile.
4. The new pages become work for Otto, built on the keywords Kiwi defined.
5. For the year-end ads, Kiwi proposes a plan with a goal, channels, a monthly cap and a cutoff rule. Nothing is spent until the owner authorizes the cap.

How Kiwi talks (illustrative numbers): "Visits from search dropped 18% from October 1 to 7 compared with the previous week. Before I propose a change, I want to see whether the drop is across every page or only the e-bike pages."

## Questions about Kiwi

### Can Kiwi change my ads without me knowing?

No. Reading and analyzing your account is different from changing it. Every new ad, launch or change needs your permission, with a spending cap, a scope and an expiration date, and every one is logged. The ad budget is yours, and Kiwi never goes over the authorized cap.

### Does Kiwi do SEO for my website?

Yes. Kiwi runs the organic search and local SEO assessment and delivers a prioritized plan. Otto writes the pages and articles from the keywords and guidelines she defines.
