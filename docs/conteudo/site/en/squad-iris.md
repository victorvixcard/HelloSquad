# Íris, AI marketing results analysis

**"I read the numbers and tell you what to do with them."**

Íris is the analyst on HelloSquad's squad, the AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses, and she handles AI marketing results analysis. She collects, consolidates, interprets and compares your marketing metrics, builds reports and recommends the next step. Always with source, period and unit.

## What Íris does

Íris shows you the number, the source and the period first, then her reading. She tells you what the data shows, what it doesn't show and what would be needed to be sure. Correlation isn't causation, and a small sample is a small sample: she says so in plain language.

With no connected source or imported spreadsheet, Íris shows no numbers. She shows you what still needs to be connected.

## What Íris delivers

| Delivery | What you get |
|---|---|
| Consolidated metrics | Indicators with source, period and unit and, when data is missing, a clear note that it's missing. |
| Performance analysis | A useful, contextual explanation of what changed, without claiming a cause without evidence. |
| Period comparison and report | A clear, reviewable report tied to your business's history. |
| Prioritized recommendations | Findings turned into actions to discuss with Nexo and the specialists. |
| Recurring executive report | A weekly summary by channel, with changes, hypotheses, suggested decisions and the source data. |
| Metric monitoring | Recurring readings and alerts, only with up-to-date, reliable data. |

## How Íris works with the rest of the squad

Íris reads Kiwi's tests, measures whether Max's campaigns brought customers back and tells Bia what the numbers say about the plan. When a finding calls for action, she recommends it and Nexo turns the recommendation into work for the right specialist.

## What Íris doesn't do

- She doesn't take any external action. Analysis is where her role ends.
- She doesn't invent data or fill gaps with estimates without labeling them as estimates.
- She doesn't attribute a sale to a channel without a configured, stated attribution model.
- She doesn't extrapolate a trend from a few data points, and she never presents outdated data as current.

## Fictitious example: Miolo's month

*Fictitious example: the brand and the numbers are made up.* Miolo is a sourdough bakery in Pinheiros, São Paulo.

1. The owner asks: "How did my month go?"
2. Íris consolidates the metrics from the connected channels and flags it right at the start: one of the sources is missing three days of data, and those days were left out of the comparison.
3. The reading: "Website visits rose 35% compared with the previous month. Behind-the-scenes posts drove more visits than offer posts. The link is likely, not proven."
4. The recommendation: publish more behind-the-scenes posts for two weeks to confirm the pattern. Nexo takes the proposal to Otto, and Íris tracks the result.

## Questions about Íris

### Can Íris analyze my marketing results?

Yes, from the sources you connect or the spreadsheets you import. She consolidates the numbers with source, period and unit, compares them with the previous period, explains what changed and recommends the next step.

### What if I don't have any data yet?

Íris doesn't make anything up. With no connected source, she tells you which data is missing and how to connect it. When a source has gaps, she shows where they are and never fills them with estimates without telling you.
