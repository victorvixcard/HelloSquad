# HelloSquad FAQ: questions about the AI marketing team

**Straight answers for anyone getting to know the squad.**

HelloSquad is an AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses. Below are the questions people ask most before they start, each with an answer that stands on its own.

## About HelloSquad

### What is HelloSquad?

HelloSquad is an AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses. Seven specialists (coordination, Strategy, Brand & Creative, Content, Growth Marketing, CRM and Results) share your brand's memory and work together. You ask in a conversation, the squad organizes and delivers, and the decisions stay with you.

### Do I need to know marketing to use HelloSquad?

No. You explain what you need in your own words, for example "I want to sell more on weekends." Nexo, the squad's coordinator, turns the request into work, asks only the questions that are still open and calls in the right specialists.

### What kind of business is HelloSquad built for?

HelloSquad is built for small and medium-sized businesses that want to organize and grow their marketing without deep knowledge of marketing tools: a bakery, a clinic, a shop. It works whether you only use social media and messaging or already have a website, ads and a customer list.

### Who are HelloSquad's AI agents and what does each one do?

There are seven. Nexo coordinates requests and projects. Bia handles strategy. Kaia creates the visuals and the visual identity. Otto writes and publishes the content. Kiwi handles Growth Marketing: ads, search engines and conversion. Max takes care of customer relationships. Íris reads the results and recommends the next step.

### How is HelloSquad different from a regular AI assistant?

A regular assistant answers one question at a time and forgets the context. At HelloSquad, specialists with defined roles work together, someone coordinates the work, everyone uses the same memory of your brand, and every request becomes a piece of work with history, versions and approval.

### Is HelloSquad a marketing agency?

No. HelloSquad is software: a team of AI agents you bring in through a conversation. The work stays on record in your business, with history and versions, and the decisions to approve, publish, send and spend are always yours.

## Working with the squad

### Can I talk to each agent directly?

Yes. You can talk to Nexo, go straight to a specialist or use the Squad Room, where everyone follows the conversation and one specialist answers at a time. When a piece of work needs several specialties, the agents collaborate on that same work.

### How do HelloSquad's agents learn my brand?

Through Brand Memory, which gathers what your business has already decided: audience, tone of voice, channels, products, words you avoid and corrections you made. Every specialist checks the same memory. What the squad notices on its own comes in as a suggestion and only becomes a rule when you confirm it.

### Do I have to connect my social media accounts to get started?

No. You can start by telling the squad what you already have: what the business sells, to whom and what it needs right now. A website, files and connected channels help the squad understand the business better, but a connection is only required to publish, send or read a channel's numbers.

### How does Autonomous Mode work?

In Autonomous Mode, you hand off a recurring responsibility, like preparing the week's posts or tracking results every day. The squad agrees with you on scope, frequency and limits, logs every cycle, and you can pause or revoke it at any time.

### Can I ask for another version if I don't like a delivery?

Yes. You can ask for a specific change, approve or turn it down. When you turn it down, you choose between canceling the work and asking the specialist for a new proposal. Every version stays in the history, with author and date.

## Control and safety

### Will HelloSquad publish posts or spend money on ads without my approval?

No. Connecting an account or requesting work doesn't authorize any action. Publishing a post, sending a message to customers and spending on ads each require a specific authorization from you and, for ads, an approved spending cap.

### What's the difference between approving and authorizing in HelloSquad?

Approving means saying a piece of material is good: some copy, a visual, a plan. Authorizing means allowing an action that leaves your business: publishing, sending or spending. They are separate decisions, and approving content never authorizes publishing it.

### Who can approve deliveries on my account?

The account owner. Other people invited to the business can follow the conversations, the work and the history, but approving, declining and authorizing external actions stay with the account owner.

### Does HelloSquad make up numbers or results?

No. The specialists only show numbers from connected sources or imported spreadsheets, always with source, period and unit. With no data, they tell you what needs to be connected. A hypothesis is labeled as a hypothesis, and a cause is only stated with evidence.

### Can HelloSquad guarantee I'll sell more?

No. HelloSquad doesn't promise guaranteed revenue growth. What it does is organize your marketing, produce professional deliveries, track real results and recommend the next step based on them.

### Will HelloSquad handle my customers or make sales for me?

No. HelloSquad handles marketing. It doesn't do sales support or negotiate deals, and it doesn't run inventory, purchasing or accounting. Max's CRM is relationship marketing, with messages only to contacts who opted in.

### Is my business data kept separate from other companies' data?

Yes. Conversations, Brand Memory, deliveries, contacts and metrics are isolated by business, and no other company ever sees anything of yours. Customer contacts only come in with their source recorded, and messages only go to people who opted in.

### What languages does HelloSquad work in?

HelloSquad works in Portuguese, English and Spanish. The language is set per business and per person, and the specialists work in your business's language.
