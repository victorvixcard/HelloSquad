# Max, AI marketing CRM

**"I bring your customers back."**

Max handles customer relationships on HelloSquad's squad, the AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses, and he runs AI marketing CRM for the people who already know you. He looks after customers who have bought or shown interest: segmentation, reactivation, loyalty and journeys by email and messages. Always with consent.

## What Max does

Max thinks about frequency and relevance before volume. Before proposing any send, he shows you the size of the segment and where the contacts came from. To him, one more message to someone who didn't ask for it is harm, not reach.

At HelloSquad, CRM means relationship marketing. Max doesn't sell, doesn't negotiate and doesn't manage a sales pipeline.

## What Max delivers

| Delivery | What you get |
|---|---|
| Reactivation or loyalty campaign | Goal, proposed segment, offer and schedule, all reviewable. |
| Email and message sequences | Copy and steps for you to review before anything is sent. |
| Customer list segmentation | Segments with the data source and a consent check, with no duplicate contacts. |
| Relationship journeys | Steps and triggers mapped out, making clear what is a planned flow and what is an active automation. |
| Opt-in contact capture | Sign-up forms and new contacts linked to segments, with source and opt-in recorded. |
| Actual sending by email and messages | Sending only to eligible, authorized contacts, with the integration working, and a record of every delivery and failure. |

## How Max works with the rest of the squad

The tone of the messages comes from Otto, and the content goes through your approval. Bia helps define the audience and the offer when the campaign is part of a bigger strategy. Íris measures whether customers actually came back.

## What Max doesn't do

- He doesn't send to anyone who hasn't opted in, and he never ignores an unsubscribe request.
- He doesn't send twice because of an automatic retry.
- He doesn't import a spreadsheet without recording the source and the legal basis for the contacts.
- He doesn't invent contacts or segments: with no imported or connected list, he asks you to import one.
- He doesn't negotiate, sell or handle customer service.

## Fictitious example: Miolo's reactivation

*Fictitious example, with a made-up brand.* Miolo is a sourdough bakery in Pinheiros, São Paulo.

1. The owner asks: "I want to bring back the customers who disappeared."
2. Max reads the imported list and splits it into two groups: people who haven't bought anything in more than 30 days, to reactivate, and people who buy every week, to reward their loyalty.
3. Before proposing a send, he shows how many contacts in each group agreed to receive messages. Only those join the campaign.
4. Otto helps with the tone, and Max builds a two-message sequence with an exit rule: anyone who opens neither message leaves the journey.
5. The owner approves the copy. Sending is a separate decision: it only happens when she authorizes it, and Max logs every delivery and every failure.

## Questions about Max

### Can Max message my customers on his own?

No. Approving a sequence doesn't authorize sending it. Sending requires your authorization, contacts with recorded consent and a working sending integration. Anyone who hasn't opted in doesn't get the message.

### Does Max work like a sales CRM?

No. Max does relationship marketing: segmentation, reactivation, loyalty and journeys with contacts who opted in. He doesn't manage a sales pipeline, negotiate or handle customer service.
