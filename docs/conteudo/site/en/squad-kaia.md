# Kaia, Brand & Creative: AI social media design

**"I shape your brand in every piece."**

Kaia is the art director on the HelloSquad squad, the AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses, and she handles AI social media design for your brand. Everything your brand shows goes through her: identity, social media visuals, in-store materials, mockups and edits.

## What Kaia does

Before creating anything, Kaia reads what your brand has already confirmed: palette, typography, visual tone, approved references and what you corrected in earlier pieces. She proposes one clear direction, not five, and explains the choice in a sentence.

When a request is too visual to put into words, she asks for a reference instead of guessing. And she never marks a piece as ready if she wouldn't approve it herself.

## What Kaia delivers

| Delivery | What you get |
|---|---|
| Static marketing visual | An image and a final file that match your brand, with preview, review and versioning. |
| Social media carousel | A consistent set of slides, reviewable as a set and ready to export. |
| Format variations and adaptations | Approved pieces adapted to other formats without losing identity or history. |
| Logo, identity and brand book | A complete flow with choices, applications and a usable file. |
| Promotional materials and packaging | Visual proposals for materials, labels and packaging, with a mockup and an exportable file. It's communication design, not product engineering. |
| Photo editing with brand references | Compositions and adjustments based on your photos and approved references, with every version kept. |
| Motion and video | Moving pieces you can review, suited to each channel, once the feature is available. |

## How Kaia works with the rest of the squad

The text inside each piece comes from Otto or from what you approved; Kaia doesn't write captions or copy. When a piece is part of a campaign, she follows the concept Bia defined. Otto handles publishing, and only with your authorization.

## What Kaia doesn't do

- She doesn't write captions or copy. That's Otto.
- She doesn't publish. Publishing belongs to Otto, with its own authorization.
- She doesn't invent products, technical specs or manufacturable packaging.
- She doesn't use third-party images, logos or photos without usage rights.
- She doesn't delete earlier versions.

## Fictitious example: Miolo's Christmas carousel

*Fictitious example, with a made-up brand.* Miolo is a sourdough bakery in Pinheiros, São Paulo.

1. On the year-end campaign project, Bia's concept "Christmas Takes Time" has been approved.
2. Kaia reads Brand Memory: a warm, direct tone and the visual references the owner has already approved.
3. She proposes a visual direction, with one sentence explaining the choice, and builds the carousel with the copy Otto wrote.
4. The owner asks to swap the photo on the second slide. Kaia creates version 2, and version 1 stays in the history.
5. Once the carousel is approved, Kaia adapts the set to the vertical format. Publishing stays with Otto, after authorization.

## Questions about Kaia

### Can Kaia create posts that match my brand's look?

Yes. Kaia uses the palette, typography, visual tone and references you confirmed in Brand Memory, and she learns from every correction. Every piece arrives with a preview, and you can review it before you approve.

### Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Kaia edits and composes from your business's photos and the references you approved, staying true to the product. She doesn't use third-party photos or logos without usage rights.
