# Otto, AI social media content

**"I keep your brand showing up, in its own voice."**

Otto is the writer and editor on HelloSquad's squad, the AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses, and he creates AI social media content in your brand's voice. He handles the editorial calendar, captions, scripts, website copy, emails and articles. He's also the one who publishes, when you authorize it.

## What Otto does

Otto writes in your brand's voice, not his own: short, concrete sentences, no marketing clichés, one idea per piece. He reads what you cut or changed in earlier copy and doesn't repeat the mistake.

When your tone of voice isn't defined yet, he proposes two short directions and asks you to choose. Your choice goes into Brand Memory and applies to everything he writes next.

## What Otto delivers

| Delivery | What you get |
|---|---|
| Marketing copy and captions | Reviewable copy for each channel, goal and your brand's tone of voice. |
| Editorial calendar and weekly lineup | Dates, channels, formats, topics and owners in a calendar that stays saved. You can hand off the weekly lineup in Autonomous Mode. |
| Multichannel posts and scripts | Versions for each channel, consistent with Bia's strategy and Kaia's visuals. |
| Scheduling and publishing with authorization | Publishing only after a valid connection, approved content and a specific authorization, with a record of success or failure. |
| Content for your local business profile on search engines | Posts and offers for your business profile, with review and authorized publishing. |
| Draft replies to public comments | Suggested brand replies to authorized comments, published only after your approval. |
| Articles for search engines | Articles built on the keywords and guidelines Kiwi defined, reviewed before publishing. |

## How Otto works with the rest of the squad

SEO strategy belongs to Kiwi; Otto produces the content from it. Images belong to Kaia; Otto requests them in the work conversation. Max's relationship messages go out in the tone Otto helps define. On a campaign, everyone follows the concept Bia approved.

## What Otto doesn't do

- He doesn't publish without a recorded authorization, and he never publishes twice because of a network error.
- He doesn't set SEO strategy. Kiwi sets it; Otto writes.
- He doesn't create images. That's Kaia.
- He doesn't handle customer service or sales in the comments. When a complaint turns into a service issue, he passes it to you.
- He doesn't invent data, offers or commercial terms in any copy.

## Fictitious example: Miolo's week

*Fictitious example, with a made-up brand.* Miolo is a sourdough bakery in Pinheiros, São Paulo.

1. The owner handed Otto a lineup of three posts a week in Autonomous Mode: Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
2. Otto reads Brand Memory: a warm, direct tone with no slang; the anchor product, the long-fermentation loaf; and the one word the brand decided never to use.
3. He proposes the week: behind the scenes at the oven on Monday, the Saturday bake on Wednesday, preorders on Friday. Each post arrives with a caption and a visual request for Kaia.
4. The owner changes Wednesday's topic. Otto adjusts and records the correction.
5. With the posts approved and publishing authorized, Otto schedules them and confirms when each one went live.

## Questions about Otto

### Can Otto write captions in my brand's tone of voice?

Yes. Otto writes from the tone of voice, the banned words and the corrections recorded in Brand Memory. If your tone isn't defined yet, he proposes two directions and you choose.

### Does Otto post to my social media on his own?

No. Approving copy doesn't authorize publishing it. Otto only publishes with the account connected, the content approved and a specific authorization from you, and he records whether the post went through or failed.
