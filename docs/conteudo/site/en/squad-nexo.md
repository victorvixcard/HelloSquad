# Nexo, AI marketing coordinator

**"I organize the squad and only call you in when the decision is yours."**

Nexo is the AI marketing coordinator at HelloSquad, the AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses. He takes your requests, figures out what you actually need, puts together the right team for each piece of work and sees it through to the end. Most requests come in through him.

## What Nexo does

Nexo turns a loose request into organized work. He identifies the goal, the context and the constraints, decides whether the request goes to one specialist or becomes a project with several steps, and passes the context along so nobody has to ask again.

When something essential is missing, he asks two direct questions at most before moving on. When the request is enough, he doesn't ask.

## What Nexo delivers

Nexo delivers organization: a clear brief, one project per goal, a single place to follow progress and decisions, and recurring routines you can pause at any time. Here is what you get from each of his four deliveries.

| Delivery | What you get |
|---|---|
| Qualified request and organized brief | Goal, context and constraints recorded and routed to the right specialist, with nothing lost along the way. |
| Coordinated project with several specialists | A campaign becomes a single project, with steps, owners, decisions and trackable deliveries. |
| Tracking and approval requests | Progress, the next decision, history and blockers in one place. No sensitive action happens without your authorization. |
| Delegated recurring responsibility | A routine agreed with you (scope, frequency and limits), with a record of every run and every failure. You can pause it at any time. |

## How Nexo works with the rest of the squad

Nexo names who's handling what: "Otto is writing the captions; Kaia comes in next with the visuals." When a request involves strategy, creative, content, ads, customer relationships and results, he calls in Bia, Kaia, Otto, Kiwi, Max and Íris in the order the delivery needs them.

He doesn't replace any specialist's technical call. If he disagrees with a choice, he records the point and lets the specialist respond.

## What Nexo doesn't do

- He doesn't write, design, analyze data or manage ads. That's the specialists' job.
- He doesn't publish, send, spend or change campaigns. Those actions need your authorization and are carried out by the specialist in charge.
- He doesn't invent a deadline a specialist didn't give, and he never marks work as done without a verified delivery.
- He doesn't take silence as approval.

## Fictitious example: Vitta Estética wants more bookings

*Fictitious example, with a made-up brand.* Vitta Estética is an aesthetics clinic in Vitória, Espírito Santo, with two people on the account: the owner and an editor.

1. The editor writes to Nexo: "I want more bookings in November."
2. Nexo asks two questions: which service the clinic wants to prioritize, and where bookings come from today.
3. With the answers, he opens the "November bookings" project: Bia builds the plan, Otto prepares the posts and Kiwi proposes ads with a spending cap.
4. Nexo shows the progress to both of them. "Approve Bia's plan" shows up in "Needs you," and the account owner is the one who approves it.

## Questions about Nexo

### Do I always have to talk to Nexo?

No. Nexo is the simplest way in, but you can talk directly to any specialist. When a request needs another specialty, the specialist tells you who handles it and Nexo organizes the rest.

### Does Nexo make decisions for me?

No. Nexo organizes the work and asks for the decisions that are yours: approving a delivery, authorizing a post, confirming a piece of information about your brand. He doesn't take silence as approval.
