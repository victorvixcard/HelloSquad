# The squad: seven AI marketing agents

**Each one owns a part of your marketing. One coordinates. You decide.**

HelloSquad is an AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses. The squad has seven AI marketing agents, each with a distinct responsibility and clear limits, and all of them work from the same Brand Memory. You can talk to Nexo, who coordinates, or go straight to any specialist.

## Who does what

Each of the seven specialists owns one area of your marketing, delivers specific work and follows written limits. Nexo coordinates; Bia, Kaia, Otto, Kiwi, Max and Íris produce. The table shows what each one delivers and what each one won't do.

| Specialist | Area | What they deliver | What they don't do |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Nexo**](/en/squad/nexo) | Projects and coordination | Qualifies the request, builds the team, organizes projects, tracks steps and asks for your decisions. | Doesn't write, design, analyze data or take external actions. |
| [**Bia**](/en/squad/bia) | Strategy | Assessment, positioning, audience, marketing plan, campaign strategy, competitor research. | Doesn't manage ads or lead SEO; doesn't produce the final piece. |
| [**Kaia**](/en/squad/kaia) | Brand & Creative | Visuals, carousels, format adaptations, visual identity, in-store materials. | Doesn't write captions, doesn't publish, doesn't use images without usage rights. |
| [**Otto**](/en/squad/otto) | Content | Captions, editorial calendar, scripts, articles for search engines and authorized publishing. | Doesn't set SEO strategy, create images or handle customer service. |
| [**Kiwi**](/en/squad/kiwi) | Growth Marketing | Media plan, ad audiences and tests, SEO, conversion optimization and experiments. | Doesn't spend without an authorized cap; doesn't set positioning. |
| [**Max**](/en/squad/max) | CRM | Opt-in segmentation, reactivation, loyalty, email and message sequences. | Doesn't sell, doesn't negotiate, never messages anyone who hasn't opted in. |
| [**Íris**](/en/squad/iris) | Results | Metrics with source and period, comparisons, reports and recommendations. | Doesn't invent data, claim a cause without evidence or take external actions. |

## How they work together

Every piece of work has **one lead**. The others join when the delivery needs their specialty: Bia sets a campaign's concept, Kaia and Otto build the visuals and the copy from it, Kiwi maps out how to promote it and Íris reads the results.

**Nexo** organizes that sequence, passes the context along and shows you the progress. He doesn't replace a specialist's technical call: if he disagrees, he records the point and lets the specialist respond.

In the **Squad Room** you talk to everyone at once, and one specialist answers at a time. In a specialist's own conversation, you talk only to that specialist.

## Why seven specialists and not just one assistant

A generic assistant answers anything and is accountable for nothing. In the squad, each specialist has one responsibility, a way of working and written limits: Kiwi doesn't spend without a cap, Max doesn't send without consent, Íris doesn't show a number without a source. That makes it clear who made each delivery, and why.

All seven share Brand Memory. What you confirm for one applies to all of them.

## What every specialist follows

- They use only what your brand has confirmed; anything they notice on their own stays a suggestion until you confirm it.
- They don't invent data, metrics, contacts or results. With no connected source, they tell you what's missing.
- Approving content doesn't authorize publishing, sending, spending or changing a campaign.
- A vague request gets a direct question, not a generic answer.
- They always keep these apart: suggested idea, prepared material, approved content, authorized action, confirmed execution and measured effect.

## Where to start

A few requests that work well as a first piece of work:

- "What do you need from me this week?" (Nexo)
- "Where can my business grow before Christmas?" (Bia)
- "Create a post for a special date" (Kaia)
- "How's the plan for next week?" (Otto)
- "Why doesn't my website show up in search?" (Kiwi)
- "Win back customers who drifted away" (Max)
- "Analyze my latest results" (Íris)
