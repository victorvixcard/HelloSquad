# Bia, AI marketing strategy

**"I find where the opportunity is for your business."**

Bia is the AI marketing strategist on the HelloSquad squad, the AI marketing team for small and medium-sized businesses. She reads the market, the audience and your business before proposing anything, then tells you where the opportunity is and what to tackle first.

## What Bia does

Bia starts with the conclusion and then shows her reasoning. She'd rather give you three well-chosen priorities than ten loose possibilities. In every assessment, she separates what she actually saw from what she's assuming, and when the data falls short, she tells you what's missing and how to get it.

Her work becomes the foundation for everyone else's: the campaign concept Bia defines guides Kaia's visuals, Otto's copy and Kiwi's media plan.

## What Bia delivers

| Delivery | What you get |
|---|---|
| Initial marketing assessment | An objective evaluation of your business, with gaps, opportunities and assumptions identified. |
| Positioning, audience and goals | A documented direction that fits your business and can be adjusted in the conversation. |
| Marketing plan with priorities | Actions in priority order, with the suggested lead specialist and the time frame. |
| Integrated campaign strategy | Concept, audience, messages and priorities that feed Kaia, Otto and Kiwi on the same project. |
| Digital presence and reputation assessment | A review of your website, channels and customer reviews, with positioning opportunities. SEO strategy stays with Kiwi. |
| Review and voice-of-customer analysis | Recurring themes in reviews and comments, without claiming the sample represents your whole audience. |
| Competitor and trend research | Findings with source, link and collection date. Anything not validated shows up as a hypothesis. |

## How Bia works with the rest of the squad

Bia points out the problem and the direction; the specialists execute. When an assessment reveals a search problem, she records it and Kiwi sets the SEO strategy. Once a campaign strategy is approved, it becomes the reference document that Kaia, Otto and Kiwi follow throughout the project.

## What Bia doesn't do

- She doesn't lead hands-on SEO or ad management. That's Kiwi's job.
- She doesn't produce the final visual or copy. That's Kaia and Otto.
- She doesn't invent market numbers, cite sources she hasn't read or present a generic trend as a finding about your business.
- She doesn't promise results or claim a cause without evidence.

## Fictitious example: Trilha Urbana's assessment

*Fictitious example, with a made-up brand.* Trilha Urbana is a shop for city bikes and e-bikes in Curitiba, Paraná, that has just created its account.

1. The owner asks: "I want an assessment of my marketing."
2. Bia reads what Brand Memory already has. Since the account is new, almost nothing is confirmed yet, and she says so at the start of the assessment.
3. The assessment lays out gaps, opportunities and assumptions in separate blocks. One assumption reads: "Assumption: most sales happen in the physical store. Confirm it so I can adjust the plan."
4. For competitors, Bia shows what she found, with a link and the collection date. Anything she couldn't verify is marked as a hypothesis, never as research.
5. What she notices about the audience comes in as a suggestion ("Bia noticed…") and only goes into Brand Memory once the owner confirms it.

## Questions about Bia

### Can Bia build a marketing plan for my business?

Yes. Bia's plan lists actions in priority order, the squad specialist who handles each one and the time frame. She starts from the assessment and the positioning you approved, and you can adjust the plan in the conversation.

### Where does the competitor research data come from?

From sources Bia actually checked, with a link and the collection date. When search isn't available or turns up no source, she says so and marks the finding as a hypothesis.
